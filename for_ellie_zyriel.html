To my Ellie, ♡

Hi baby,

I wanted to make you something that says a little more than just “I love you.” Because those three words never seem big enough for how I feel about you.

I keep thinking about what you told me: “Relationship isn’t a race.” You’re right, baby. I don’t want to rush you or rush us. I want to take my time learning you, laughing with you, and making beautiful memories together.

I love your little “baby” messages, hearing about your day, and knowing you think of me even when you’re busy with your own things. You have your own life, your work, your research, and people you love. I want to be a peaceful, happy part of that life, not another thing you have to worry about.

We’ve both been hurt before. Sometimes I let my fears speak louder than my heart, and I’m sorry for the times that made you feel pressured. You deserve patience, trust, kindness, and respect. That’s the kind of love I want to show you—not just in words, but in the way I treat you.

I can’t wait for all the pictures we still have to take, our random calls, our silly conversations, and all the ordinary days that might become our favorite memories.

So no rushing, baby. Just us, getting to know each other better, one day at a time.

I love you so much, Ellie Zyriel. ♥— your baby
